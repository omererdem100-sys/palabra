## Notable corrections and distinctions

- **cheque / cuenta:** the notebook gives cheque as a restaurant bill; corrected to bank cheque. Restaurant bill is cuenta. [Banco de España](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/menu-horizontal/productosservici/efectivocheques/cheques/guia-textual/concepto_cheque/Caracteristicas.html) describes the banking document.
- **estrenar:** includes using or wearing something for the first time, beyond simply wearing. [Dictionary reference](https://www.spanishdict.com/translate/estrenar).
- **el sol:** corrected handwritten el sal beside Turkish güneş (sun); salt remains la sal.
- **mayor, despertar, mazorca:** corrected handwritten major, desperter and mazarco. Accents and standard spelling normalized throughout.
- **calmar:** to calm, rather than simply to be silent; **callar** covers keeping quiet/silencing.
- **haber:** auxiliary have / there to be, rather than ordinary possession (tener).
- **disculpar, divertir, sentar, bañar:** distinguished from their reflexive forms. Notebook glosses sometimes imply disculparse, divertirse, sentarse and bañarse.
- **dañar / deber:** debt/obligation notes continue below dañar but belong with deber; dañar means damage.
- **legumbre:** pulses/legumes in the intended Spain context, more precise than generic vegetables.
- **requesón:** fresh curd cheese, not exactly spreadable cream cheese.
- **letra:** written letter/character or lyrics, rather than generic word.
- **actitud:** attitude; **fino:** fine/delicate; **salón:** large room/lounge, with regional classroom context explained in the row.
- **ser seguro / estar seguro:** safe or reliable versus being sure, with construction-dependent overlap noted.
- **tortilla, coger, tiquete, carro, papas, prender, mozo/mesero:** regional meanings and Spain usage are noted rather than silently replaced.
- **carta, sueño, banco, lengua, hoja, obra, rico, fresco:** multiple meanings are retained where relevant. English answers show the target senses explicitly.
